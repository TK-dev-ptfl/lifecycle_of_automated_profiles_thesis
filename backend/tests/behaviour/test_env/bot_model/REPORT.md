## Ablation: without raw-timing features

The same forest refitted without log_raw_rate, raw_dt_cv, raw_dt_tiny_frac, raw_step_cv - the features computed on raw event timing, which could encode the recording device rather than the hand. If the held-out numbers hold up, the model separates humans from bots by the path itself.

## Caveats

- Synthetic bots reuse each SapiMouse stroke's own endpoints, point count and timestamps, so they differ from the human stroke only in path shape and velocity profile. Their positions are rounded to whole pixels like real ones. The Iliou bots are real automation, which is why their held-out numbers matter more.
- Phase 2 Iliou humans contributed two sessions each and are grouped by session, not by person.
- Datasets were recorded with real mice at their own rates; Playwright input arrives through DevTools at roughly one event per frame. Resampling removes most of that difference, not necessarily all.
